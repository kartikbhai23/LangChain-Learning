## The Spirit of Cricket

With bat and ball, the game begins, A world of passion, where everyone wins.

A soaring six, a wicket's fall, Cricket brings excitement to all.

From village grounds to stadium lights, Players chase their dreams and fight.

From Sachin's grace to Kohli's fire, Cricket fills our hearts with desire.

Win or lose, we stand as one, The game continues, never done.

More than a game, a bond so true, Cricket lives in me and you!
